# Para Officinal

## Register

product

## Users

Authenticated Para Officinal customers managing orders, delivery tracking, favourites, support, profiles, and addresses; and internal administrators operating catalogue, customer, fulfilment, loyalty, reporting, and configuration workflows.

## Product Purpose

Provide a dependable Moroccan parapharmacy storefront and operations platform. Authenticated surfaces should help customers complete account tasks confidently and help staff execute dense operational workflows quickly, without changing the public shopping experience.

## Brand Personality

Clinical, refined, and reassuring. The product should feel precise and trustworthy, with a restrained premium quality suitable for a professional pharmacy business.

## Anti-references

Avoid oversized marketing CTAs inside product surfaces, glossy neon treatments, gaming-style effects, heavy glow, detached circular arrow modules, excessive pill shapes, inconsistent button systems, and decorative motion that distracts from work.

## Design Principles

1. Make action hierarchy immediately understandable.
2. Keep operational controls compact, familiar, and consistent.
3. Use restrained emerald accents to communicate priority, not decoration.
4. Preserve existing workflows while improving visual clarity.
5. Isolate authenticated product styling from the public storefront.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA where practical. Preserve visible keyboard focus, semantic controls, minimum 40 × 40 px interactive targets where required, reduced-motion support, readable disabled states, and accessible names for icon-only actions.
